 The basic idea of ensemble learning is derived from the idea of *wisdom of crowd*.
**`Wisdom of crowd- the idea that the collective judgment or opinion of a large, diverse group of independent individuals is often more accurate and insightful than that of a single expert`**
This same applies for ml models, if you train multiple models on a training set and predict the class using all the models then predict the class with highest vote.
Random Forest work on this same principle, it create a forest of multiple Decision Forests. 
Ensemble models are very useful, majority of use cases involve ensemble methods.
After we try out few good predictors in end of the project we will use ensemble methods to combine them into a even better predictor.
