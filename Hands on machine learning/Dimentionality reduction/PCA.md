PCA is a very complex algorithm in sense of mathematics, the basic idea of this algo is to convert high dimension data and breakdown it into low dimensions.
It find principle axis for the data for which the variance is maximum.
Variance tells how spread my data is from mean, 

Population Variance
$$
V = \frac{\sum(x_i - mean)^2}{n}
$$
Sample Variance(small part of large data)
$$
V = \frac{\sum(x_i - mean)^2}{n-1}
$$
