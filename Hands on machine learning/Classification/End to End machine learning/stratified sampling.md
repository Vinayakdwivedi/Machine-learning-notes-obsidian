Let's take an example, imagine you are conducting a research on people of India where there are 51% male and 48% female so if you select people randomly there is 16% chance that your sample is skewed, so to prevent that, let's say we are taking 100 people for study, from which there will be 51 people will be male and 48 will be female to make the sample stratified. We create different homogenous groups called <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">strats</mark> like we did above in male female example.
